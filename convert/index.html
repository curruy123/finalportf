Methodology & caveats

This analysis is based on 247 ranked games from a single player's Akali history. All findings should be treated as exploratory hypotheses about personal decision patterns, not generalizable claims about Akali as a champion. Economic differentials are measured against the enemy mid laner using position data from the Riot Match Timeline API (60-second snapshots). "Deaths while ahead" requires ≥300g gold lead at time of death. With a sample of 247 games, no claims of statistical significance are made.
